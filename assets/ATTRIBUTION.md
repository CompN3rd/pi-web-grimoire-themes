`textures/{cream,parchment,foxing}.svg` are original procedural artwork created
for this repository, distributed under its MIT license (see `../LICENSE`).
They contain static noise, fibres, and foxing; no external resources or scripts.
Paper wear at the edges is drawn with CSS. No third-party texture photographs
are used. Fonts and textures are served locally; no runtime network dependency
on Google Fonts or any other external service.
